# Northstar Cloud Incident Response Runbook

## Suspected incident

1. Confirm whether the issue is reproducible and whether more than one customer is affected.
2. Check Datadog latency, error rate, deployment markers, and affected endpoints.
3. Notify the incident owner and open an incident channel.
4. Use a reversible mitigation first. Rollback requires the incident owner's approval.
5. Send a customer update with impact, current mitigation, and next update time.

## After resolution

Record the timeline, customer impact, detection gap, root cause, permanent fix, and follow-up owner.
